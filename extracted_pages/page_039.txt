Red Flag Signs
These signs should be assessed before initiating treatment for need for management/
consultation through modern medicine.
• Secondary bacterial or viral infection (pus, fever)
• Generalized erythroderma
• Growth delay in children with Atopic Dermatitis
• Severe psychological distress
(A) Preventive Treatment
The effective management of Dermatitis (Vicharchika) focuses on both preventive and curative
measures, emphasizing diet, lifestyle and psychological well-being. Preventive measures
involve Nidana Parivarjana, which refers to the avoidance of causative factors of the disease.
Diet
It is recommended that one’s Satmya Ahara (habitually suitable and agreeable food) be taken
warm and fresh, avoiding preparations that are excessively dry or heavy. The food should
be consumed mindfully, with proper chewing and at a steady, moderate pace. The intake of
Viruddhahara (incompatible foods) may contribute to the onset of skin diseases; therefore,
such foods should be avoided.
Life style
Following Dinacharya (daily regimen) and Ritucharya (seasonal regimen) as prescribed in
classical Ayurvedic texts helps in maintaining overall health and preventing diseases
