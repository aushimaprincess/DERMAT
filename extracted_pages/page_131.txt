• Urticaria with symptoms of anaphylaxis.
• Hereditary angioedema, physical urticaria not responding to treatment.
AT LEVEL 2: (CHC/Small hospitals (10-20 bedded hospitals with basic facilities such as
routine investigations, X-ray)
Clinical Diagnosis: Same as Level 1
Non-responding cases of mild to moderate severity and severe cases shall be referred to
level 2 and 3. The case referred from Level 1, or a fresh case must be evaluated thoroughly
for any complications.Identification of patient specific etiopathological factors (nidana), dosha
involvement (Initial kapha-vata dominance may start showing signs of Pitta aggravation) and
assessment of Rogibala is needed before initiation of treatment.
Investigations: The diagnosis would be primarily clinical. Investigations may be necessary (if
not done at primary level) to investigate complications or exclude other differential diagnoses
as follows:
• Infection screens
• Thyroid Panels
Management
The second level management of Sheetapitta includes Shamana as well as Shodhana
Chikitsa—a purificatory approach used when symptoms are chronic, recurrent, or associated
with Bahudosha Avastha (excessive doshic aggravation).
Purification/other procedures
1. Vamana (Therapeutic Emesis)
• Decoctions of Patola, Nimba, Vaca used for emesis
• Eliminates Kapha and Ama from the Amasaya
• Indicated when itching and swelling are predominant
2. Virechana (Purgation Therapy)
• Triphala Churna, Trivrit Avaleha, Navakarshika Kashaya
• Targets Pitta and Rakta Dushti
• Especially useful when burning sensation (Daha) and redness (Raga) are prominent
Recommended Follow-Up Period
Immediate Post-Shodhana Phase (Days 1–7)
• Frequency: Daily or alternate days
• Focus: Monitor Samyak Lakshana (signs of proper purification), bowel movements,
hydration, and vitals
• Care: Begin Samsarjana Krama (gradual diet restoration) with Manda, Peya, Vilepi
Recovery Phase (Weeks 2–4)
• Frequency: Weekly
• Focus: Assess reduction in symptoms like Kandu, Daha, Shotha
• Care: Introduce Shamana Chikitsa if needed, continue dietary regulation
