CASE DEFINITION 1, 2
Psoriasis is a common, chronic, inflammatory and proliferative condition of the skin and may
be associated with systemic manifestations in many organ systems. The most characteristic
lesions are red, scaly, sharply demarcated, indurated plaques, particularly over the extensor
surfaces and scalp. It can cause itching, irritation, burning and stinging, and the extent and
severity vary enormously over time and between individuals.
