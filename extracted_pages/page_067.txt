Evaluation and Assessment
The Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI is a practical 10-question tool assessing the
impact of skin disease on patients’ lives, scored from 0 to 30, with higher scores indicating
greater impairment.26
The Dermatology-specific Quality of Life (DSQL) instrument measures physical,
psychological, and social limitations through 36 items, focusing on disease-specific impacts.27
Skindex-16 is a validated 16-item questionnaire scored on a 0–6 Likert scale, evaluating the
effects of skin diseases on quality of life, with lower scores indicating better QoL28.
