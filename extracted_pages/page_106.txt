i. Deepana and pachana- with Shaddharana churna or Hingwashtakachurna70or
Vaishwanarachurna71 or Ajamojadichurna72or Bhaskara lavanachurna73- 3-6 twice daily
before food or Chitrakadi gutika74.for a duration of 3 days or till the patient have good
appetite.
ii. Snehapana- with Panchatiktakaghrita or Aragwadhaghrita or Tiktakaghrita or
Mahatiktakaghrita - starting from 30 ml till samyaksnigdha lakshana (in arohana doses
increased according to agni). Duration of 3 to 7 days. Sadya snehapana is indicated in
children and adolescents.
iii. Abhyanga and Swedana- Eladi kera or Nalpamaraditaila or Nimbaditaila or any
suitable oil as per dosha involvement indicated for Kustha or twakroga followed by
ushnambusnana. Duration of 3 days.
iv. Vamana- with Madanapippali, Nimba, Vacha, Saindhava and Madhu (or) Vacha
Yashtimadhu Madanapippali in Nimba swarasa with honey (or) Any vamana yoga
specifically indicated for skin disease. Duration: One day.
v. Samsarjana krama- Peyadi karma as per avara, madhyama or pravarashuddhi for 3/5/7
days.
vi. Virechana- with Nimbamritadierandataila, Avipattikara churna or Avipatti choorna,
Trivrit choorna or Triphalakwatha or other virechana drugs according to the dosha
predominance. Other internal medicines are stopped during virechana and restarted
during or after samsarjana krama.
vii. Samsarjana krama after virechana as per avara, madhyama or pravarashuddhi for 3/5/7
days. During snehapana, vamana, virechana and samsarjana krama period other internal
medicines are stopped.
viii. Sarvanga Kashaya dhara. With Aragwadhadikashaya or Manjishtadikashaya or
Panchatiktaka Kashaya or any twakdoshahara and raktaprasadana kashaya.
ix. Takradhara- with Musta Amalakitakra or Aragwadhaditakra or Manjishtaditakra or
Panchatiktakatakra for 7 days. Takradhara may be extended to 14 or 21 days as per
dosha status. In children, Shirolepam may be done instead of dhara. When shirolepam is
done, amalaki and buttermilk prepared in kashayasiddha ksheera are used and Talam is
also done with Rasnadi or Kachuradichurna with Dhanvantaram taila75 or Bala taila76or
Nimbamritaditaila.
x. Prachhana/ Raktamokshana/ Siravedha: As per locations of lesions on lalata, hasta
pada or shiras. Prachana is done in alpadosha status. In bahudosha, Siravedha is advised.
Jalooka can be applied in children.
• All internal medicines are continued till dosha and rogashamanaalso considering
upashayaanupashaya.
• Rasayana drugs are started after dosha and rogashamana and continued for a
period of one to 6 months.
• Ayurvediya Swasthavritta is to be followed during treatment and continued after
complete rogashamana.
