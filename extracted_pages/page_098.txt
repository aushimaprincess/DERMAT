*These are general pathya-apathya guidelines to be followed in skin diseases. However, they
may be tailored on a case-to-case basis, taking into account the patient’s prakriti, type of
Kustha, seasonal variations, and other relevant factors.
B. INTERVENTIONS
At Level 1: Solo physician clinic/health clinic/PHC (Optimal standard of treatment where
technology and resources are limited).
Psoriasis cases with mild to moderate severity, BSA<10 can be treated in level 1. BSA>10,
non-responding cases of mild to moderate severity and severe cases shall be referred to level
2 and 3. The varieties of psoriasis like guttate, erythrodermic psoriasis, generalized pustular
psoriasis, psoriasis with co-morbidities, associated depression, cardiometabolic risks may also
be referred to level 2 and 3.
Fastly spreading psoriasis with acuteness of symptoms as in visarpa, where continuous
monitoring is needed shall be referred to level 2 and 3. Psoriasis with bahu doshavastha with
recurrences and relapses shall be also referred to level 2 and 3.
Clinical diagnosis: Psoriasis is entirely diagnosed clinically with thorough medical history
and physical examination, including BMI. However, some investigations, such as complete
hemogram, urine routine/microscopic, C-reactive protein, fasting blood sugar, postprandial
blood sugar levels, and serum uric acid levels can be advised.
Management:
Prevention protocol:
Diet: Ayurveda based dietary advisories for prevention of skin disease can be followed as part
of primary prevention.
Lifestyle: Life style advocacies based on Ayurveda sleep regimen, vegadharana, promotion
of vyayama, following proper dinacharya with udwarthana therapy (powder massage) and
rithucharya to prevent seasonal exacerbations.
Curative Intervention:
At level 1, curative intervention is solely based on Shamana protocol based on dosha
predominance of clinical manifestation.
Plaque psoriasis –Vata kapha Kustha (charma Kustha, eka Kustha, kitibha)-
• In active phase( pro-inflammatory phase),Manjishtadi kwatha ,Punarnavadi kwatha,,Vilwadi
gutika,Dooshivishari gutika, Arogya vardhini gutika,Avipatti churna,Nishunti shadanga
(Shadanga excluding sunthi) ,Chandrakala Rasa etc can be advocated.
• Mahamanjishtadi kwatha and Patolakaturohinyadi kwatha can be given in the sub acute
phase.
• Ayyappala kera taila, Dinesavalyadi kera oil, Jeevanthyadi yamaka, Vajraka ghrtha and
Mahātikta Ghṛta can be advocated as Shamana medicine in vatakapha phase.
• The external applications shall be withheld in the pittanubandha conditions as in acute
phase and shall be only applied after inflammation has been subsided.
