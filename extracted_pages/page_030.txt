Clinical Presentation of Vicharchika 13
Clinical presentation of Vicharchika includes Kandu/ Atikandu (Itching),Pidika (Papular
eruption), Shyava (Blackish/greyishdiscolouration), Bahusrava (Excessive/copious discharge),
Lasikadhya (Excessive watery discharge), Atiruja (Excessive pain) and Raji (Manifestation of
linear lesions)
CLINICAL EXAMINATION14
A detailed patient’s history should be taken and focus on the following:
• Onset and distribution of lesions
• Inspection to identify skin rashes, redness, dryness, scaling, crusting, discolouration,
thickening and other signs of irritation.
• The severity of pruritus (e.g., keeping patient awake at night)
• Family and/or personal history of the atopic triad. (Atopic triad refers to the genetic
predisposition to develop allergic conditions such as atopic dermatitis, allergic asthma,
allergic rhinitis and other allergies like conjunctivitis)
• Presence of contact allergens
• The presence of triggers including allergens (e.g., dust mites, animal dander), hot showers/
sweating, soaps, fragrances, food hypersensitivities, and synthetic fabrics such as polyester.
