Clinical Diagnosis
The diagnosis of tinea is primarily clinical and made after physical dermatological examination.
However, some investigations, like a complete hemogram, and KOH mount may be done.
Assessment according to Dermatology Life Quality Index (DLQI), Dermatology-specific
Quality of Life (DSQL), SKINDEX-16.
At level 1, diagnosis is based on clinical signs and symptoms i.e. Kandu(itching/pruritus)
Raga(redness/erythema), Atasi pushpavat pidika (macular rashes similar to flax flower)
Tamra varna pidika (copper-coloured macular rashes), Visarpanipidika (diffused macular
rashes), Dirgha Pratana (tendril-like macular rashes), Unnata mandala – Pari Mandala
(Annular lesions).
Management
At level 1, curative intervention is solely based on the Shamana protocol or Mrudu Shodhana
with Haritaki, Triphala, etc. Internal & External Ayurvedic formulations, as mentioned in Table
No 9 can be used.
