Hand Hygiene:
• Washing hands frequently with soap and water, especially after touching potentially
contaminated surfaces or individuals.
• Don’t Share towels, clothing, razors, and personal care items.
Foot Hygiene:
• Wear breathable shoes and moisture-wicking socks, especially in warm or humid weather.
• Dry feet thoroughly, particularly between the toes, after showering or swimming.
• Avoid Walking Barefoot in Public Areas:
Clothing Choices:
• Opt for loose-fitting, breathable clothing made of natural fibers like cotton, which can help
prevent moisture buildup.
• Allow shoes to dry out completely between uses to prevent fungal growth.
Nail Hygiene:
• Keep nails short and clean, and avoid biting or scratching the skin, especially if you have
an existing fungal infection.
Hygiene in Communal Areas:
• Wash Exercise Clothes After Each Use.
*These are general pathya-apathya guidelines to be followed in skin diseases. However, they
may be tailored on a case-to-case basis, taking into account the patient’s prakriti, type of
Kustha, seasonal variations, and other relevant factors.
Follow Up: Once in every 15 days or as needed, for effective management of the condition.
Referral Criteria
• No response at all after 2-3 weeks of treatment period.
• Uncontrolled comorbidities like diabetes mellitus, hypertension etc.
• Evidence of an increase in severity/complications
• Diagnostic uncertainty.
• Substantial impact on their quality of life and activities of daily living.
At Level 2: (CHC/ Small hospitals 10-20 bedded hospitals with basic facilities such as routine,
investigation, X-ray)
Clinical Diagnosis: Same as level 1. The case referred from Level 1, or a fresh case must be
evaluated thoroughly for any complications.
Investigations: The diagnosis would be primarily clinical. However, some investigations may
be necessary to investigate complications or exclude other differential diagnoses as follows:
• Renal function test.
• Fasting lipids, glucose, and thyroid functions.
Management: If there is Bahudoshatwa, the patient may be admitted to a Level 2 facility for
Snehapana, Shodhana, Rakta Mokshana, with constant monitoring and observation.
