Samprapti / Pathogenesis
Prakupita Vata and Kapha (Pradushta Kapha Maaruta) due to Sheeta Maarutadi Nidana and
Sheeta Maaruta Samsparsha (exposure to cold air and objects) when being amalgamated
with Pitta (Pittena Saha Sambhooya) spreads internally and externally (Bahir Antah Visarpah)
and results in Sheetapitta-Udarda-Kotha.20
