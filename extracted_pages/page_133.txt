Follow Up
Immediate Post-Shodhana Phase (Days 1–7)
• Frequency: Daily or alternate days
• Focus: Monitor Samyak Lakshana (signs of proper purification), bowel movements,
hydration, and vitals
• Care: Begin samsarjana krama (gradual diet restoration) with Manda, Peya, Vilepi
Recovery Phase (Weeks 2–4)
• Frequency: Weekly
• Focus: Assess reduction in symptoms like Kandu, Daha, Shotha
• Care: Introduce Shamana Chikitsa if needed, continue dietary regulation
Maintenance Phase (Weeks 5–12)
• Frequency: Biweekly to monthly
• Focus: Prevent recurrence, monitor Agni, Bala, and skin health
• Care: Use Rasayana therapy (e.g., Haridrakhand, Guduchi, Triphala) to build immunity
Referral Criteria:
• Same as mentioned earlier at Level 1 &2
• Other modalities can be considered depending on the case.
