Diet 40
Emerging evidence suggests that nutritional modulation may aid in vitiligo prevention and
management by supporting melanocyte health and reducing oxidative stress. A diet rich in
antioxidants, vitamins, and minerals is recommended.
• Recommended dietary components:
• Antioxidants (Vitamin C, E, β-carotene, selenium, zinc)
• Folic acid, Vitamin B12, and Copper (important for melanogenesis)
• Phytochemicals (from fruits, vegetables, green tea, turmeric)
Lifestyle 41
Lifestyle practices that reduce physical and psychological stress are vital, as stress is a
known trigger for vitiligo onset and progression.
• Avoidance of skin trauma (e.g., tight clothing, cosmetic irritants) to reduce Koebner
phenomenon
• Stress management techniques such as meditation, mindfulness, and counseling
• Adequate sun protection to prevent photodamage of depigmented skin
• Maintenance of sleep hygiene and regular circadian rhythms
Yoga 42
Yoga and related practices have shown beneficial effects in vitiligo, primarily through modulation
of the immune system, stress reduction, and enhancement of systemic antioxidant status.
Some of the practices found helpful are as follows:
• Pranayama (breathing exercises)
• Surya Namaskar (sun salutation)
• Shavasana, Yoga Nidra for relaxation
• Meditation to reduce anxiety and autoimmune activation
• (Proper guidance from a yoga expert is essential before practicing yoga for skin
disorders)
