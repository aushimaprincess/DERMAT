Clinical Diagnosis
The diagnosis of Dermatitis (Vicharchika) is primarily clinical and made after complete medical
history and physical examination, however, following investigations may be done to assess
severity of inflammation or to exclude other diagnosis:
• Complete Hemogram
• Skin scraping with KOH
Assessment of severity by different assessment tools such as SCORAD, ADSISASSAD,
DLQI
