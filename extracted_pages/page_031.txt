• Infants rarely have atopic dermatitis lesions affecting the diaper area but may be susceptible
to other causes of diaper dermatitis such as candida or seborrheic dermatitis.
• Atopic dermatitis children classically have fewer exudative patches and plaques on
antecubital and popliteal fossae; adults have chronic lichenified (enhanced skin markings)
lesions that have a predilection for hands.
• Individual lesions may also be further classified into acute (oedematous, erythematous
papules and plaques and/or vesicles/crusting), subacute (erythema, scale, variable
crusting), or chronic (thick plaques with lichenification and scale) stages.
Clinical diagnosis as per Ayurveda
Darshana (Inspection) – Site of manifestation, Pidika, Shyava, Bahusrava etc
Sparshana (Palpation) – Rukshata, Kharata etc
Prashna (Interrogation) - Desha, Vaya, Kaal, Kandu, Ruja etc
Pariksha:
1. Dushya – Dhatu, Mala (Sapta Dushyas of Kustha - Tvaka, Rakta, Mamsa, Lasika, Tridosha)
2. Desha - Site of manifestation/ geographical habitat
3. Bala – Pravara/Madhyam/ Avara
4. Kala– Season/ condition of Skin (Navam/ Purana)/ Sanchaya, Prokopa, Prasara,
Sthanasanshraya, Vyakta, Bheda Avastha
5. Agni –
• Jatharagni - Mandagni/ Samagni/ Tikshagni/ Vishamagni and Pravara/ Madhyam/ Avara
• Dhatavagni - Pravara/ Madhyam/ Avara
6. Prakriti - Sharirika and Manasika Prakriti
7. Vayas – Balya/ Madhyama/ Jirna
8. Satva - Pravara/Madhyam/ Avara
9. Satmya – Rasasatmya/ Okasatmya and Pravara/ Madhyam/ Avara
10. Ahara – Digestive capacity
Other factors to be considered before finalizing the treatment:
• Dosha Involvement – Vata, Pitta, Kapha
• Involvement of Raktadushti, Dushivisha, Garavisha
• Vata – Anuloma/ Pratiloma
• Srotodushti
• Misbehaviours/ Misdeeds done in the past may cause guilt feeling/ stress that can initiate
or precipitate a Kusthasamprapti – This may be enquired during history taking.
• Dhatus affected – From the symptoms enumerated for each Dhatugata Kustha, and also
from the symptoms listed for each Dhatu Dushti, the Dhatus affected are to be inferred
and treatment should be planned.
