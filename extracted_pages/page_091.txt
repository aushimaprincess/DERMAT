CLINICAL EXAMINATION21
Psoriasis is mostly diagnosed on the basis of clinical examination of the skin, scalp,and
nails along with review of patients smoking history, medical history and family history. The
characteristic skin lesions are sharply demarcated, scaly, erythematous raised plaques, either
painful or itching red patches covered with silvery scales. The lesions vary in shape and may be
ovoid, round, or irregular and are often symmetrically distributed. Common areas of involvement
include extensor surface of body, elbows, knees, scalp, trunk, lumbosacral region and nails. A
skin biopsy needed in some cases to confirm the diagnosis and to rule out other diseases.
Ayurveda clinical examination comprises of darshana, sparshana and prashna pareeksha
which provides leads to dosha predominance of clinical manifestation of psoriasis.
Dashavidha Pareeksha (Tenfold examination) are also included in Ayurveda clinical
examination to formulate adequate treatment protocol.
