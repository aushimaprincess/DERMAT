(B) Curative Treatment
Treatment can be initiated from the Poorvaroopa Stage itself. Preferably combinations/
formulations containing Kusthahara property may be selected.
Stages of treatment:
• Elimination of Ama and kleda (process of digesting and eliminating toxins (Ama) from
the body) by Pachana, Anulomana or Sramsana; Patolamooladi Kashaya25 and Phalatrikadi
Kashaya 26 etc may be used for Sramsana/Anulomana.and Chitrakadi gutika26 may be used
for Pachana.
• Agnideepana (concept of stimulating and strengthening the digestive fire ie, Agni)
Examples: Agnitundi Vati27 Ajamodadi churna28, Shaddharana Churna 29etc.
• Strotovishodhana (process of cleansing and purifying the body channels (Srotas) to
remove blockages and promote proper circulation) such as Trivruta Churna 30
• Vyadhiviparita Chikitsa
Such as Manjisthadi Kashaya31 ,Mahamanjisthadi Kashaya, Panchatikta Guggulu Ghrita32,
Khadirarishta, Sarivadyasava 33etc.
• Rasayana (Rejuvenation and promoting longevity through specific treatments and lifestyle
practices)
Such as Mahatiktha Ghrita34, Madhusnuhi rasayanam35, Bhallataka Rasayanam36, Gandhaka
rasayanam37 , Khadirarishta etc.
• Krimi, Visha, Visarpa and Jwara Chikitsa as per the condition.
Formulations such as Nirgundyadi Kashaya38,Vidanga Churna, Vilwadi Gulika,39 Dushivishari
agada40, Dasanga lepa43, Visha Taila41 etc can be used.
• Nidana Parivarjana and Pathya Sevana
Pathya needs to be followed during and after the treatment.

• Vata Pradhana – Sarpipan/ Ghritapana

• Kapha Pradhana – Vamana
