Pathya/ Do’s Apathya / Don’ts
Vihara- Apathya
1.Vegavaraodha (Suppression of natural urges)
1. Atapasevana (Over exposure to Sun)
2. Drinking cold water after direct exposure to the sun and after exertion.
3. Consuming food before the complete digestion of the previous meal.
4. Day sleep
5. Stress
6. Doing exercise or over exertion immediately after heavy meals
7. Over exertion after Snehapana & Vamana
8. Sudden change from cold to heat or heat to cold without following the rules of gradual
change
9. Sudden change from Santarpana to Apatarpana& Vice-versa
10. Improper administration of Snehapana and Panchakarma Procedures
Line of treatment
At level 1- (Solo Physician Clinic/Health & Health Clinic/PHC (Optimal Standard of
treatment in situation where technology and resources are limited)
Clinical Diagnosis
• Clinical Criteria-Observation of well-demarcated, milky-white macules or patches
Check for symmetry, sensation and common sites (face, hands, genitals, etc.)
• Family History - Enquire about similar conditions in family
• Provisional Diagnosis- Based on the history and presence of acquired depigmented
patches
In most cases, no laboratory investigations are required unless there are clinical signs
suggestive of associated autoimmune or endocrine disorders. Patient counselling, reassurance,
and early initiation of treatment can be effectively initiated.
