SUPPORTIVE INVESTIGATIONS 1
• Diagnosing psoriasis is typically based on clinical presentation
• A skin biopsy will be helpful in atypical cases.
• In generalized pustular psoriasis, high sensitivity C-reactive protein may be mildly elevated,
but higher levels are seen in psoriatic arthritis.
