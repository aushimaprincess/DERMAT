Red Flag signs
These signs should be assessed before initiating treatment-
• Secondary bacterial infection.
• Intractable alopecia cases causing permanent hair loss in tinea capitis
• Uncontrolled Diabetes-mellitus
• Lower extremity cellulitis in cases of tinea pedis.
• Underlying severe immunodeficiency due to HIV/AIDS.
• Severe wide spread infection causing significant pain or inflammation or eczematization
irrespective of treatment.
(A) Prevention Management:
Avoidance of the etiological factors mentioned in Kustharoga nidana(food, dietary habits,
lifestyle and behavioural characters) is the most important preventive measure.
Other preventive measures:
• Following Dinacharya, Ritucharya, and Sadvritta.
Yoga
Yoga practice plays a supportive role by:
• Boosting immunity
• Reducing stress. (which can worsen fungal infection)
• Enhancing blood circulation to the skin.
• Supportive detoxification and hormonal balance.
Yoga can play a supportive role in prevention by enhancing overall immunity and promoting
skin health. Regular practice of asanas like Shavasana, Padmasana, Sidhasana, Makarasana,
and Suryanamaskara improves blood circulation and detoxification, helping to maintain
healthy skin. Pranayama and meditation reduce stress, which is known to lower immunity
and increase susceptibility to infections. Shat kriya and yoga nidra helps in deep relaxation for
immune system repair.
