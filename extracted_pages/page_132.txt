Maintenance Phase (Weeks 5–12)
• Frequency: Biweekly to monthly
• Focus: Prevent recurrence, monitor Agni, Bala, and skin health
• Care: Use Rasayana therapy (e.g. Haridrakhand, Guduchi, Triphala) to rebuild immunity
Referral Criteria
• Same as mentioned earlier at Level 1
• Failure to respond to initial medical management/ Acute exacerbation of the disease
• Hereditary angioedema
• Physical urticaria not responding to therapy
• Any other life-threatening complications
• Clinical diagnosis referral from second-level to third-level is considered when symptoms
intensify or become chronic, requiring deeper detoxification.
AT LEVEL 3: (Ayush hospitals attached with teaching Institution, DistrictLevel/ Integrated/
State Ayush Hospitals)
Clinical Diagnosis: Same as Levels 1 & 2.
Confirm diagnosis and severity with the help of following investigations.
• Screening for neutrophil rich infiltrates in skin biopsy, performing gene mutation analysis
for hereditary periodic fever syndromes.
• Autologous Serum Skin Test (ASST)
• In cases of suspected hereditary angioedema (HAE), test for complement C4 levels, If mean
wheal duration >24 hours, perform biopsy of skin lesion to assess for signs of urticarial
vasculitis.
• Complement activity tests.
• Anti-nuclear antibody, rheumatoid factor, and/or anti-citrullinated protein.
• Thyroid autoantibodies to TSH receptor, thyroglobulin, thyroid peroxidase and sodium/
iodine symporter.
• Biopsy to be done when there is doubt regarding the diagnosis (lesions persisting beyond
24 hrs, non-blanchable, fever, joint pains etc.)
• This level is applied when the disease is chronic or relapsing and there is significant doshic
involvement (especially Pitta and Rakta)
Management
• The third-level management of Sheetapitta includes raktamoksha along with other
sodhana therapies.
