Differential Diagnosis in Ayurveda
Differential diagnosis of Sheetapitta should be made from Udarda, Kotha/Utkotha, Visarpa
and Kotham -Kustha.
Sheetapitta is associated with picking pain and itching, aggravated at evening time. The
differentiation is given as:
• Udarda: considered a Tridoshaja Vyadhi, Kapha produces kandu, Vata induces toda and
Chardi and jvara by Pitta with Kapha predominance, distinguishing it from Sheetapitta,
which has Vata-Pitta dominance. Skin eruptions look alike, but Kapha dosha is predominant
; Udarda is usually aggravated at morning and sometimes it looks like skin colour.
• Kotha/Utkotha: features like itching and swelling are more prominent. In Kotha eruptions
are numerous in number and small in size 24
• Visarpa: Rapidly spreading skin lesions, often with systemic symptoms.25
• Kotham-Kustha: not an acute condition- discoloration, scaling, oozing, and localized
skin disease; Sheetapitta is more acute and episodic. Kotham (Kustha/skin disease, often
psoriasis or eczema-like) is considered a Kapha–Pitta dominant disorder with deeper tissue
involvement.26
