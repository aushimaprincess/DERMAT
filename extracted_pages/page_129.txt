Clinical diagnosis: Sheetpitta /Urticaria is diagnosed after a complete medical history,
psychiatric, triggering factors and physical examination. Some investigations, like CBC
(Complete Blood Count), ESR (Erythrocyte Sedimentation Rate), CRP (C-Reactive Protein),
Stool Examination, total IgE may also be done.
