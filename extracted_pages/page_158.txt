Referral Criteria
Patients with vitiligo should be referred to a higher-level facility (Level 2 or 3) under the
following circumstances:
• Uncertain Diagnosis – Atypical presentation or diagnostic confusion with other
hypopigmented disorders (e.g., leprosy, pityriasis alba, tinea versicolor).
• Rapid Progression – Sudden and extensive spread of depigmented lesions.
• Mucosal or Extensive Involvement – Vitiligo affecting genital mucosa, lips, eyelids, or
involving 10% of body surface area.
