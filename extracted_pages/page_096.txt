Red Flags
These signs should be assessed before initiating treatment for need for management
through modern medicine.
• Patients with generalized pustular psoriasis or erythrodermic psoriasis with high grade
fever.
• Psoriatic arthritis significantly impacting a person’s physical, psychological or social well-
being.
• Psoriasis with co morbid disorders
(A) Prevention Management
Diet
Ayurveda dietary directives termed as Aṣṭāhāra-vidhi-viśeṣa [29] principles are essential in diet
planning for prevention of psoriasis for high-risk group patients. It is also advised that one’s
satmya ahara (habitual, agreeable food) be served warm, fresh, neither overly dry nor heavy,
and consumed mindfully with thorough chewing at a measured pace.
Life style
Ayurveda emphasizes adherence to dinacharya (daily routine) and ritucharya (seasonal
routine), with particular attention to following dinacharya in the correct sequence.
Yoga
Various yoga practices may be helpful in the management of psoriasis, such as Shavasana
(Corpse Pose), Bhujangasana (Cobra pose), Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose), Trikonasana
(Triangle Pose), Balasana (Child’s Pose). Certain Pranayam and meditation techniques like
Anulom Vilom (Alternate Nostril Breathing), Bhramari (Humming Bee Breath), and Kapalbhati
(Skull Shining Breath) will also help in reducing anxiety, emotional stress, cellular healing,
reducing stress-related inflammation and promoting a positive mental state.
