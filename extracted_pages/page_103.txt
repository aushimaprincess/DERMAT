Purification/ other procedures (As per applicability)
Since purificatory procedures like vamana and virechana require Shodhana snehapana under
strict medical supervision, in level 1, feasibility issues will be there for implementation of
Shodhana protocol. Sadya snehapana followed by sadyovamana, siravedha can be planned
in OPD basis under strict medical supervision.
Follow up: Once in every 15 days or as needed, for effective management of the
condition.
Frequent and long term follow up is needed to monitor psoriasis patients to prevent
recurrence and monitor remissions and exacerbations, if any.
Referral Criteria
• A PASI score of 5 to 10 is considered moderate disease, and a score more than 10 is
considered severe with substantial impact of quality of life and daily living.
• Generalized pustular psoriasis or erythrodermic psoriasis patients can be referred to a
higher centre for specific management.
