The specific treatment protocol advised in the management of the Shvitrarogais as follows:
Special Shodhana in the Treatment of Shvitra (Vitiligo)
3-4days
Administration of Malpurasa* + mixed with SnuhiKsheera**
– For Sransana
Massage with mustard oil (Sarshapa Taila Abhyanga), followed by
Surya Pada Santapa (exposure to sunlight)
Only Peya (thin rice gruel) should be taken for 3 days
(Taken whenever the patient feels thirsty)
Formation of Eruptions (Sphota) on Shvitra Patches
When eruptions appear, they should be opened or punctured
Afterwards for 15 days:
– Daily morning intake of Kwatha (decoction) of Malpurasa, Vijayasara, Priyangu, and
Shatapushpa or Palasha Kshara Kwatha with Phanit (sugarcane syrup).
– Kusthaghna herbs are beneficial and should be taken with or after Khadira Kwatha/Jala
daily.
