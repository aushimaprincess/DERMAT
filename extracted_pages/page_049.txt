• Deepana and pachana- with Shaddharanam churna or Hingwashtaka churna56 or
Vaishwanara churna57or Ajamojadi churna or Bhaskara lavana churna for a duration of
3 days or until the appetite is good.
• Snehapana- with Panchatiktaka ghrita or Aragwadha mahatiktaka ghrita or Tiktaka
ghrita or Mahatiktaka ghrita - starting from 30 ml till Samyaka snigdha lakshana is
observed (for a maximum period of 7 days).
• Abhyanga and Swedana- Eladi kera or Nalpamaradi taila58 or Nimbadi taila or any
suitable oil as per Dosha involvement indicated for Kustha or Twak roga followed by
Ushnambu snana for 1 day.
• Vamana- Vamana karma may be done with Madanapippali, Nimba, Vacha, Saindhava
and Madhu (or) Vacha Yashtimadhu Madanapippali in Nimba swarasa with honey (or)
Any other Vamana yoga specifically indicated for skin disease. Duration: One day.
• Samsarjana krama- Peyadi Krama should be suggested as per Avara, Madhyama or
Pravara shuddhi for 3/5/7 days respectively
• Virechana- with Nimbamritadi Eranda taila, Avipattikara churna, Trivruta leha, Triphala
kwatha59 as appropriate taking doshavastha into consideration .Other internal medicines
are stopped during Virechana and restarted during or after Samsarjana Krama.
• Samsarjana krama after Virechana as per Avara, Madhyama or Pravara shuddhi for 3/5/7
days.
• Sarvanga Kashaya dhara. Can be done with Aragwadhadi kashaya or Manjishtadi
kashaya or Panchatiktaka kashaya or any other Twakdoshahara and Raktaprasadana
kashaya.
• Takradhara - with Musta Amalaki takra or Aragwadhadi takra or Manjishtadi takra or
Panchatiktaka takra for 7 /14/21days depending on the Dosha status. When Shirolepam
is done, Amalaki and buttermilk prepared in Kashayasiddha Ksheera are used. Rasnadi
choorna/Kachuradi churna with Dhanvantaram taila/ Bala taila / Nimbamritadi taila can
be used for tailam
• Marsha nasya- with Ksheerabala taila or Shadbindu taila. Takradhara, Shirolepa and
Nasya help in alleviating anxiety and stress which is usually a comorbidity in all psoriatic
patients.
• Prachhana/ Raktamokshana/ Siravedha: As per locations of lesions, Raktamoksha is
done on Lalata, Hastapada or Shiras. Prachana is done in Alpadosha status. In Bahudosha,
Siravedha is advised. In children Jalooka can be applied.
All internal medicines are continued till Dosha and Roga Shamana. Upashaya Anupashaya
may be considered.
Rasayana drugs are started after Dosha and Roga Shamana and continued for a period of one
to 6 months.
Swastha vritta is to be followed during treatment and continued after complete Roga Shamana.
