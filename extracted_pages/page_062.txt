Examination involves examination by Darshana (Inspection), Sparshana (Touch/Palpation)
and Prashna (history taking).
A. Darshana Pareeksha (Inspection) Appearance, extension and dimensions of visible
disease should be observed.
B. Sparshana Pareeksha (Palpation)- Generally Sparshana Pariksha is not required.
C. Prashana Pareeksha (Questioning)- A physician must ask about nature, intensity,
aggravating factors, onset, duration and frequency of disease, Agni, Bala, Aahara-Vihara
and other factors to assess the Roga and Rogi Bala.
