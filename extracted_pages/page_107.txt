• Pathya aims to eliminate factors that trigger or worsen the condition, promoting healing
and balance within the body.
Follow Up: Once in every 15 days or as needed, for effective management of the condition.
Strict Pathyaapathya are to be continued for a period equal to or two times the total
panchakarma treatment period during follow up.
Naimittika rasayanas should be given on follow up.
Frequent and long term follow up is needed to monitor psoriasis patients to prevent recurrence
and monitor remissions and exacerbations, if any.
Referral Criteria
• Same as mentioned earlier at level 1, plus
• Failure of acute exacerbation to respond to initial medical management.
• In erythrodermic patients with generalized pustular psoriasis, patients with febrile and
unstable vitals or secondary infections.
• Cases with prominent joint damage and marked functional impairment.
• Uncontrolled complications such as Cardiovascular disease, Cancer, HIV, metabolic
syndrome, psychiatric illness or hyperuricaemia.
• Any other complications that threaten the life of the patient.
