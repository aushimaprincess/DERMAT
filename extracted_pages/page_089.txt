Psoriasis affecting specific sites
Scalp psoriasis • The commonest area affected by plaque
psoriasis is often the site first affected, and
multiple discrete plaques of varying sizes
diffusely involve the whole scalp.
• Presented as dandruff-like flaking and
silvery-white scale, with severe itching
leading to burning sensation or soreness and
bleeding.
Sebopsoriasis15 • Plaques of thin, sharply demarcated
erythema with variable scales may occur in
the typical areas of seborrhoea, like paranasal
areas, external ears, medial eyebrows,
hairline, preseternal, and interscapular chest
wall.
Flexural psoriasis • Flexor psoriasis is often seen in individuals
(Inverse psoriasis) with plaque psoriasis elsewhere on the skin
• Most common sites- axilla, submammary
folds, umbilicus, inguinal creases, and other
body folds- are commonly seen in older
adults and are associated with obesity
Genital psoriasis • In males, the most commonly seen in the
glans penis, followed by scrotal skin and
penile shaft, associated with inverse or
plaque psoriasis.
• In females, most commonly seen vulval

erythematous, non-scaly, well-demarcated
