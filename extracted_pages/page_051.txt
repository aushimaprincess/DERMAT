Referral Criteria
• Same as mentioned earlier at level 2, plus
• Life-threatening complications, e.g., sepsis from superimposed infection.
• Treatment-refractory cases unresponsive to Ayurveda and supportive therapy.
• Severe psychological morbidity which needs inpatient psychiatric care.
• Integrated approach for cases with psychiatric distress or systemic complications.
• Uncontrolled immunosuppressive complications such as infections like Staphylococcus
aureus, eczema herpeticum (HSV), and molluscum, ocular complications such as cataracts
and keratoconus, telangiectasia, and acneiform eruptions, hypertension and nephrotoxicity
etc.
• Suspicion of malignancy or precancerous skin changes (e.g., cutaneous T-cell lymphoma)
• Requirement of immunosuppressants or systemic antibiotics.
• Other modalities can be considered depending on the case.
