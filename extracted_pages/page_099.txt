Guttate psoriasis, Generalized Pustular psoriasis, Inverse psoriasis- Pittakapha pradhana
Kustha:
This variant of pittakapha kustha is hyper acute generally and hence the pittakapha hara
kashayas like Vasaguloochyadi and Padolamooladi kashayam are preferred choices, alongside
Avipatti choornam, Amritashadanga panakam, Kaisora guggulu and Amrita guggulu . In
non- exudative phase Durvaditaila, Nalpamarditaila etc. can be used externally. In case of
associated Jwara, Sudarshana Gutika and Mustaparpataka panakam can be added. Thiktaka
ghrita and Mahathiktaka ghrita can be advocated when active phase of inflammation is over.
Note: If Ama is either minimal or addressed with brief deepana–pachana, a small, supervised
samana-sneha of Mahatikta Ghrta / Tiktaka Ghṛta / Pancatiktaka Ghrta (≈ 10 mL, b.i.d.) is
beneficial even in acute phases (कुवष्ठनं नितेहपानतेन पूिवं सिचिमुपाचरतेत्| AH-19/1)
Palmoplantar psoriasis–
Treatment principle of vipadika can be adopted. In exudative and bleeding phase, the medicines
mentioned in acute phase of vata kapha Kustha can be used. In dry vata kapha phases, non-
exudative non-painful presentation, medicines mentioned in sub-acute/chronic phases can
be advocated. The external applications in subacute and chronic phases include jeevanthyadi
yamaka and mahathiktaka ghrita lepa.
Scalp psoriasis:
The treatment principle is similar to plaque psoriasis. External scalp applications include
Ayyappala kera taila and durvadi taila.
Psoriatic arthritis (Vatarakta):
It is seen associated in 30% of psoriasis cases. The treatment principle includes the treatment
protocol of plaque psoriasis and vatarakta treatment protocol including Mahamanjishtadi
kwatha, shaddharanam churna/tablet and Simhanada guggulu for arthritic management.
Manibhadra gula can be used internally in all varieties of psoriasis when active inflammatory
phase is over.
