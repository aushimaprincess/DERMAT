CLINICAL EXAMINATION
Clinical examination of urticaria involves assessing the patient’s overall health, including vital
signs, identifying potential triggers (as mentioned in tables 1 & 2), and performing a physical
examination of the urticarial lesions.
The physical examination of urticaria includes15
• Type/Appearance of lesion.
• Size
• Shape
• Colour of lesion
• Margin
• Number
• Site of lesion
• Distribution-whether lesions are localized or generalized.
