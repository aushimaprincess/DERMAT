Shamana therapy involves both internal and external formulations, such as kwatha, arishta,
churna, ghrita, taila, and lepa.
In psoriasis, bahirparimarjana chikitsa (external therapy) is particularly beneficial when the
vitiated doshas are cleared at the level of deeper dhathus and get localized at the level of
the twak (skin)[34]. In cases of bahu dosha avastha (excessive dosha aggravation), Shamana
chikitsa is administered after Shodhana therapy, to pacify the remaining doshas.
Satvavajayam:
Satvavajaya chikitsa or psychotherapy in Ayurveda is indicated through dhee, dhairya and
atmaadi vijnaanam. This has a major role in correcting psoriatic complications including
psoriatic depression.
