Follow Up: Clinical examination needs to be done during every visit- atleast once in every
15 days or earlier as per need. Strict adherence to dietary restrictions (Pathya apathya) for
a duration equal to or twice the total period of treatment should be advised. Naimittika
rasayanas should also be administered during follow-up visits. Screening for complications or
any other secondary infections evidenced by Atiruja, Atilasikasrava etc.
Referral Criteria
• Lack of clinical improvement after 4-6 weeks of individualized treatment
• Extensive skin involvement (>30% BSA), especially in children or elderly.
• Severe pruritus leading to sleep disturbance or psychological distress.
• Severe pain with burning or stinging sensation.
• Secondary infection suspected due to oozing, crusting, fever, or lymphadenopathy.
• Diagnostic uncertainty (e.g., differential diagnosis from psoriasis, seborrheic dermatitis,
scabies).
• Evidence of an increase in severity/complications.
• Coexisting systemic diseases (diabetes, hypothyroidism, renal impairment) affecting skin
healing.
• Recurrent relapses despite compliance with therapy.
• Suspected allergic etiology needing patch testing or further evaluation.
• Substantial impact on their quality of life and activities of daily living
