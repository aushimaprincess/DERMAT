• Shamana Chikitsa (Pacification)
The Shamana Chikitsa in the Shvitraroga precedes the Shodhana Chikitsa in most cases. The
Chikitsa is aimed at the pacification of the residual doshas after purification and is carefully
planned as per the evaluation. The common drugs used in the management are as follows:
