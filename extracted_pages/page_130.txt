FollowUp: In the first-level management of Sheetapitta, which primarily involves Nidana
Parivarjana (avoiding causative factors), intake of shamanoushadhis and dietary regulation, the
follow-up period is relatively short and focused on early symptom resolution and prevention
of escalation.
Initial Phase (Days 1–7)
• Frequency: Every 3–5 days
• Purpose: Monitor response to initial interventions
• Focus: Reduction in itching (Kandu), swelling (Shotha), and redness (Raga)
Stabilization Phase (Weeks 2–4)
• Frequency: Weekly
• Purpose: Ensure sustained relief and prevent recurrence
• Focus: Evaluate digestion (Agni), skin condition, and adherence to Pathya-Apathya
guidelines
Transition Criteria to Second-Level
If symptoms persist beyond 2–3 weeks or recur after initial relief or new symptoms like Daha,
Jwara, or Chhardi appears, the patient may be referred to level 2.
Referral Criteria
• If symptoms cannot be controlled with regular treatment and even after avoidance of
triggering factors.
• Patients suffering from angioedema.
• Substantial impact on their quality of life and activities of daily living.
• Evidence of an increase in severity/complications
• Diagnostic uncertainty.
• Uncontrolled comorbidities such as diabetes mellitus, hypertension etc.
