Some principles that determine the investigation are:
1. Acute urticaria is generally not investigated.
2. Acute recurrent urticaria is frequently clarified with a history, as a possible trigger (food,
medication, activity) can be identified. One should not forget to enquire about mucosal
contacts, such as toothpaste, latex condoms, or even semen. Everything that comes in
contact with the mucosa should be sought for, like drugs (prescription and over-the-
counter; ask directly about aspirin and nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs [NSAIDs]),
all foodstuffs including spices, drinks and even chewing gum.
3. Chronic urticaria often remains idiopathic. Now there is a concept of endotypes and there
are certain investigations that can be employed to differentiate the types of CSU, but they
relate to the course and preferred treatment and need expert interpretation by centres
that are proficient in these tests. Certain drugs like anti histamines, Omalizumab and BTK
inhibitors are prescribed on the basis of these tests and endotype classification.

as above in view based on the best current evidence [Table 6 and 7]. Provocation tests for

Table 6: Investigations for Acute Urticaria

6. Oral drug challenge tests
*ONLY if IgE mediated Type 1 hypersensitivity is suspected as a cause of urticaria, avoid
