(A) Prevention Management35,36
 Hydration: Maintain adequate water intake to support skin hydration and overall health.
 Avoid known triggers. (Nidana Parivarjan)
 Regular moderate exercise: Promotes immune balance.
 Sleep and environmental controls: Ensure proper sleep hygiene; maintain cool, well-
ventilated surroundings; choose breathable, loose cotton clothing.
 Bath and change of clothes: If pollen or animal contact has triggered urticaria in the past,
take a bath and change clothes.
Yoga37
Regular practice of yogic techniques such as Neti (nasal cleansing), selected Asanas—
including Tadasana (Palm Tree Pose), Trikonasana (Triangle Pose), Veerabhadrasana I & II
(Warrior Poses I & II), Setu Bandhasana (Bridge Pose), and Shavasana (Corpse Pose)—along
with breathing practices (Pranayama) such as Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing),
Bhramari (humming bee breathing), and Sheetali (cooling breath), play a significant role in
maintaining overall health.
These practices collectively help in reducing psychological stress, improving blood circulation,
facilitating detoxification, and regulating immune function. Furthermore, the inclusion of
Dhyana (meditation) and Yoga Nidra (yogic relaxation) enhances mental stability and promotes
psychosomatic balance. Together, these interventions contribute to strengthening the body’s
natural defense mechanisms and may aid in reducing the risk or progression of disorders such
as Urticaria.
