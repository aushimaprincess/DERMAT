• Morbidity includes chronic pruritus, secondary infections, sleep disturbances, and

• Key risk factors across these types of dermatitis include genetic predisposition, atopic
background, impaired epidermal barrier, exposure to irritants or allergens, microbial
dysbiosis, emotional stress, and environmental factors.
• Dermatitis may be entirely endogenous (constitutional) or be entirely exogenous (contact).
The latter consists of irritant and allergic contact reactions. Commonly, dermatitis
has a multifactorial aetiology and may be aggravated by the presence of pathogens
(staphylococcus aureus). Assessment of the relative importance (contribution) of the
possible factors may be difficult and subjective.
Dermatitis/eczematous conditions can be considered under Vicharchika in Ayurveda.
Vicharchika is a disease caused by the vitiation of tridosha with the predominance of
Vata and Kapha. Acharya Sushruta mentions a Vicharchika type with Pitta dominance
also, where there is minimal discharge, excessive itching and pain, and linear skin-lesions
(अलपस्ािावतकण्डिरत्चिरुजाव्ितरावजमती विचरचचिका वपत्तेन ). As per Kustha Samprapti, the three doshas
provoked by the particular nidanas creates shaithilya of Twak, Rakta, Mamsa and Lasika
Dhatus. Further vitiation of doshas causes accumulation in the Shithila Dhatus resulting in
Dosh Dushya Samoorchana. Based on the level of dosha-dushya involvement, various skin
diseases such as Vicharchika are manifested. Dushivisha as well as Raktadushti may also play
roles in manifestation of Kustha including Vicharchika.
