Referral Criteria
Patients at Level 2 should be referred to higher centers under the following conditions:
• Same as mentioned earlier at level 1, plus
• No clinical improvement or worsening despite appropriate homoeopathic management.
• Complications requiring advanced diagnostic tools or specialty care.
• Need for multidisciplinary evaluation or emergency care.
• Non-responsiveness in suspected autoimmune, systemic, or rapidly progressing
pathology.
• Diagnostic uncertainty requires investigation beyond the available infrastructure.
• Referral should be made to Ayush Level 3 or appropriate higher-level healthcare
facilities with specialist services and comprehensive diagnostic setups.
• Any other complications that threaten the life of the patient.
Level 3- (Ayush hospitals attached with teaching Institution, District Level/Integrated/State
Ayush Hospitals)
Clinical Diagnosis: Same as Level 1 & 2.
Confirm diagnosis and severity with the help of investigations such as:
• Thyroid function tests (TSH, fT3, fT4), anti-thyroid antibodies, ANA, fasting glucose levels,
HbA1c.
• Wood’s lamp examination can be used to enhance the visibility of depigmented lesions,
particularly in fair-skinned individuals or early-stage disease. If needed, differential diagnosis
from other hypopigmented conditions (e.g., pityriasis versicolor, post-inflammatory
hypopigmentation) can be aided by routine investigations and clinical pattern recognition.
(In case of clinical suspicion or doubt)
