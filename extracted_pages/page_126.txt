DIAGNOSTIC CRITERIA
The diagnosis will be done based upon the clinical presentation, clinical examination and
investigations as mentioned above.
• Ayurvedic Diagnostic Approach:
• Nidana (Etiological Factors): Includes exposure to cold breeze, incompatible foods
(Viruddha Ahara), and seasonal triggers.
• Dosha Assessment: Predominant involvement of Vata and Pitta with Rakta and Rasa as
affected dhatus.
• Twak Vikara Evaluation: Observation of skin lesions, itching (Kandu), burning sensation
(Daha), and swelling (Shotha).
• Rogabala and Vyadhibala: Strength of the disease and the patient’s immunity are
considered for treatment planning.
PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT
The principles of management include assessment of signs and symptoms before initiating
treatment and the need for management through conventional treatment for associated co
morbidities. If the patient is already under standard care, the physician may advice to continue
the same along with add-on Ayurvedic treatment and can be assessed for the same in the
follow ups for tapering or discontinue the treatment in consultation with the conventional
physician.
Red Flag Signs
These signs should be assessed before initiating treatment for need for management/
consultation through modern medicine.
• Angioedema of airway structures & family History.
• Respiratory distress (stridor, wheezing).
