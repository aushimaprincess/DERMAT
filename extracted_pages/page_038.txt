PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT
The principles of management include assessment of signs and symptoms before initiating
treatment and the need for management through conventional treatment for associated co
morbidities. If the patient is already under standard care, the physician may advise to continue
the same along with add-on Ayurvedic treatment and can be assessed for the same in the
follow ups for tapering or discontinue the treatment in consultation with the conventional
physician.
In Ayurveda, Kustha is an umbrella term for various skin diseases. It results from improper diet
and lifestyle that significantly affect physiological and psychological well-being. The causative
factors hint the involvement of Ama visha, Krimi, Rakta and Manas in addition to the Tridoshas.
All the skin diseases are said to be Tridoshaja and is generally considered as chronic.The
permutations and combinations of Nidana decide the Dhatus and Doshas involved, leading to
specific skin diseases.
Management may include Nidana Parivarjana (avoidance of disease-causing factors),
Samprapti Vighatana (the process of disrupting the pathogenesis (Samprapti) of a disease
to facilitate its treatment and to facilitate non-recurrence) and relevant principles of Krimi,
Visha, Visarpa and Jwara Chikitsa. The clinician should be able to diagnose the doshas and
dooshyas involved and the level of involvement to decide the selection of drugs and duration
of treatment.
