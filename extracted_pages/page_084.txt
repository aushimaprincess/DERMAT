PATHOGENESIS AND ITS THERAPUETIC IMPLICTIONS5
1. Genetics:
There are two peaks of onset of psoriasis (Fig.). Type I has onset in the second and third
decades and a more common family history of psoriasis. Type II has onset in late adulthood
in patients without obvious family history.
2. Hyperproliferation
The growth fraction of the upper layer of skin known as the epidermis is increased with the
basal cell being increased 7x compared with normal skin. The cell turnover time decreases
from 30 to 5 days, similarly the cell cycle time reduces from 311 to 36 hours.
3. Inflammation
Psoriasis is now known to be a autoimmune disorder with CD4T cells, Th17 and Th 12
cells playing an important role.8 Notably these cells are determined by cytokines that
are the target of novel drugs known as biologic which have changed the therapeutic of
psoriasis.9 [fig] Psoriatic keratinocytes contain high levels of antimicrobial peptides which
may account for the surprisingly low incidence of bacterial skin infection.
4. Psoriasis is also now appreciated to be an intimate part of the metabolic syndrome,
associated with hypertension, diabetes mellitus, and coronary artery disease.
