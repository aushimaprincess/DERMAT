At Level 3: Ayush hospitals are attached with teaching institutions, District Level/ Integrated/
State Ayush Hospitals, Tertiary care allopathic hospitals having Ayush facilities, and multiple
departments/ facilities for diagnosis and interventions. Additional facilities like dieticians,
counselling, and physiotherapy must be provided.
Clinical Diagnosis: Same as Levels 1& 2. Confirm diagnosis and severity with the help of
investigations such as skin biopsy (only done for cases in which clinical diagnosis is not clear),
Further investigations like inflammatory bio markers, ECHO, MRI, CT scan, and chemical
analysis of uric acid renal stones, Fibroscan(to detect early liver damage in patients with a
history of methotrexate used for 2-3 years and alcohol consumption) etc may be advised as
per individual case needs.
Management-
The treatment strategy planning at level 3 is also based on dosha dominance and includes
purificatory therapies for bahudosha Kustha, and subsequent rasayana medicines for
strengthening the purified dhathu, as mentioned in Level 2. In the case of madhyama dosha
or alpa dosha Kustha, shamanoushadhi will be utilized as per dosha dominance and dhathu
involvement. The medicines that can be utilized in the treatment of psoriasis is given in Table No:
9. The treatment of psoriasis will not be complete without nidana parivarjana or identification
and avoidance of etiological factors which may vary from individual-to-individual.
Management of psoriatic complications:
• In metabolic complications associated with psoriasis including metabolic syndrome,
NAFLD, anemia and liver cirrhosis, patolamooladi kwatha, patolakaturohinyadi kwatha,
vasa guduchyadi kwatha, arogyavardhini vati can be advocated
• In psychiatric complications associated with psoriasis, the snehana samanoushadha
including kalyanaka ghrita, maha kalyanaka ghrita; manasa mitravataka, takra sirodhara
etc can be advocated.
