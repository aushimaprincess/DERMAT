Message
India's rich heritage of traditional healthcare systems has long provided holistic, time-tested
solutions for the prevention, management, and promotion of health. In recent years, there has been growing
recognition of the significant role that Ayush systems can play in addressing the increasing burden of skin
diseases through patient-centric, and integrative healthcare approaches.
The release of the Standard Treatment Guidelines (STGs) for Dermatological Disorders in the
respective Ayurveda, Siddha, Unani, and Homeopathy (ASUH) systems, with the inclusion of Yoga, marks
another important milestone in our collective efforts to strengthen the integration of Ayush systems into the
national healthcare framework. Building upon the success of the previously released STGs, these
guidelines reaffirm our commitment to harmonizing the wisdom of traditional medicine with contemporary
scientific evidence, thereby enhancing the quality, safety, and accessibility of healthcare services.
These guidelines provide evidence-based recommendations for the prevention, diagnosis, and
management of common dermatological disorders, including Psoriasis, Dermatitis, Vitiligo, Urticaria, and
Dermatophytosis. By offering standardized and system-specific clinical guidance, they will support
healthcare practitioners in delivering safe, effective, and holistic patient care while promoting rational and
evidence-informed clinical practice.
I am confident that these STGs will contribute significantly to improving clinical outcomes,
encouraging integrative models of healthcare, and strengthening the role of Ayush systems in Public health
sector. They will also serve as valuable reference documents for clinicians, academicians, researchers, and
policymakers, fostering greater uniformity and excellence in patient care across the country.
I wholeheartedly appreciate the dedicated efforts of Ayush Vertical under DGHS as well as all the
experts, institutions, researchers, and stakeholders whose invaluable contributions have made the
development of these comprehensive guidelines possible. I am confident that this initiative will further
strengthen the vision of accessible, affordable, and quality healthcare for all through the Ayush systems of
medicine.
(Prataprao Jadhav)
14th August 2026
New Delhi
