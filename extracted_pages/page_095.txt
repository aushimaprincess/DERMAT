Classification of severity and prognostic assessment
• Psoriasis area severity index (PASI) score [26]
The PASI is a widely used instrument in psoriasis trials that assesses and grades the
severity of psoriatic lesions and the patient›s response to treatment. It produces a numeric
score ranging from 0 to 72. A 75% reduction in the PASI score (PASI 75) is the current
benchmark for most clinical trials in psoriasis.
• Nail psoriasis Severity index. [27]
This scale is used to evaluate the severity of nail bed psoriasis and nail matrix psoriasis
by area of involvement in the nail unit. The NAPSI will be useful during clinical trials for
evaluating response to treatment of psoriatic nails.
PRINCIPLES OF MANAGEMENT:
The principles of management include assessment of signs and symptoms before initiating
treatment and the need for management through conventional treatment for associated co-
morbidities. If the patient is already under standard conventional care, the physician may
advice to continue the same along with add-on Ayurvedic treatment and can assess/consider
for tapering or discontinue the treatment in consultation with the conventional physician in
the follow ups. Must check about the intake of drugs that can trigger or exaggerate psoriasis
in the history of the patient.
According to Ayurveda, Kustha is a disease that arises due to improper diet and lifestyle
(mithya ahara and vihara)[28], along with certain causative factors (nidana) that also impact
psychological aspects. So, in the management of psoriasis, both prevention and curative
aspects concentrate on diet, life style and psychological component.
