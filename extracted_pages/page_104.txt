At Level 2: (CHC/ Small hospitals (10-20 bedded hospitals with basic facilities such
as routine investigation, X-ray)
Clinical Diagnosis:
The case referred from Level 1, or a fresh case must be evaluated thoroughly for any
complications. Identification of patient specific etiopathological factors (nidana) in diet and
lifestyle, classification based on dosha involvement and staging of the disease based on dhatu
involvement should be done to plan the treatment accordingly.
Investigations: The diagnosis would be primarily clinical, along with some investigations
which will be necessary to investigate complications or exclude other differential diagnosis
as follows:
• Complete blood count/ ESR
• Renal function Test, liver function test,
• Fasting lipids, glucose, and thyroid functions
• Complete urine examination
• High sensitivity C-reactive protein.
• Serum electrolytes
• X-ray of affected Joints (Psoriatic arthritis)
• Tests for Biologics is referred to a specialized tertiary level center
Management
OPD/IPD level management:
In addition to the management mentioned in Level 1, staging and classification of the psoriasis
based on dhatus affected and doshas involved should be done at level 2 to determine the
prognosis and mode of management.
The sadhya (curable) Kustha roga affecting only rasa, rakta and mamsa can be treated at OPD
level and yapya (managed with strict diet and regimen but not completely curable) Kustha
roga which affects medodhatu should be treated in a hospital with constant follow up to
prevent recurrence.
The cases in which only alpadoshatwa, is present may be treated at the OPD level with
identification of specific nidanas, nidanaparivarjana, vicharanasnehapana, shodhana,
naimittikarasayana and sesha dosha shamana. When the psoriatic lesions are limited to skin,
it can be treated under the line of treatment of Kustha rogas and raktavritavata.
When Shodhana is done, the bala of the patient should be taken into consideration. In vata
predominant conditions, snehapana and anulomana with Kusthaharasnehas are advised and
madhyama and pravarashodhana are avoided. Vamana is indicated in psoriasis with kapha
predominance and those affecting upper parts of the body (example, scalp psoriasis). Virechana
